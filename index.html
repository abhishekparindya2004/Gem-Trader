ABOUT GEMTRADER

Sri Lanka's Premier Digital Gemstone Marketplace

GemTrader bridges Sri Lanka's legendary gemstone heritage with the modern global marketplace. Our platform connects gemstone buyers, collectors, exporters, and verified sellers through a secure, transparent, and professional digital trading experience.

From the world-famous Ceylon Blue Sapphire of Ratnapura to rare Ruby, Spinel, Cat's Eye, and other precious gemstones, GemTrader helps buyers discover authentic treasures backed by certification, trust, and industry expertise.

Built on transparency, authenticity, and innovation, GemTrader empowers Sri Lankan suppliers to reach international markets while giving global buyers the confidence to trade with verified local professionals.
